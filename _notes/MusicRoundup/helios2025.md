Helios' light banishes the darkness, and I really needed a summer of calmer healing. Despite long hours working, I believe this was successful! No big vacations or big dramatic events, just a simple summer working, theatering, and spending time with my people. As always, here's the semester top 5:

5. CRAZY NOISY BIZZARE TOWN by THE DU - It earned this spot on sheer hype value alone. I haven't even watched JoJo's Part 4 yet! It's just such a fantastic energy song, and it never fails to get me moving.

4. Calling After Me by Wallows - I'm sad I missed Wallows at ACL the only time I went, because I've just recently discovered them and they are fantastic. This is definitely my favorite of their songs, calm and energetic and coy all at once.

3. The Night We Met by Lord Huron - I have often complained that no other folk music captures the same vibes as my boy Gregory Alan Isakov. In steps Lord Huron, who managed to address all my complaints and more with this beautiful song.

2. Oh Lord Two by Lincoln and Pollyanna - Lincoln is, as he addresses in his newest album, overshadowed by Saint Bernard, despite most of his discography (this included) being much, much better than it. Oh Lord Two is a haunting but warm melody that has stuck with me so hard this entire summer.

1. Presumably Dead Arm (617 Sessions) by Sidney Gish - An unconventional love song that fights itself on that label, with an immaculate sense of flow between its lyrics. It's absolutely unforgettable and easily wins my top song for this summer.

Honorable Mention: I Wanna Be Your Girlfriend by Ezra Furman - My partner introduced me to Ezra Furman, and I'm deeply glad they did. This song was just barely edged out of the top 5. It's a very pretty song with heaps of personality, just like Furman's other work.

Short List:
Gold on the Ceiling by The Black Keys, Why by Dominic Fike, Good Girls (Don't Get Used) by Beach Bunny, Chocolate by The 1975, Achilles Come Down by Gang of Youth, Loving Machine by TV Girl, Banks by Lincoln, We Are Young by fun., CRAZY by LE SSERAFIM, i'm low on gas and you need a jacket by Cavetown, Baby Take My Acid by Lincoln and Penelope Scott, Sofa King by Royel Otis, Dance, Baby! by boy pablo, Situation by Yazoo, Cherry Blossoms by Shady Moon, Turn & Go & Turn by Everyone Everywhere, and boom by bbno$.
